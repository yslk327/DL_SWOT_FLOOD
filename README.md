This study systematically reviews the application of Deep Learning (DL) to flood forecasting and mapping, examining scientific trends, methodological advances, and future opportunities. Following PRISMA guidelines, relevant studies were analyzed using Bibliometrix and Biblioshiny to identify publication trends, key contributors, and thematic clusters. A SWOT analysis further assessed the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats of DL-based approaches. Results highlight the strong performance of CNN, U-Net, and LSTM models, alongside rapid growth in research, particularly in Asia and Europe. Key challenges include limited generalizability and interpretability, while hybrid modeling and cloud-based deployment offer promising directions for operational flood management.
